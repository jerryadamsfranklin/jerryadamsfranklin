## About me

I build production-grade intelligent systems, from fine-tuning LLMs and orchestrating AI agents to shipping decentralized ML at scale. At Digital Currency Group, I architected **VCScout-AI**, a LangChain-based agentic RAG pipeline with Qwen-7B and Weaviate that analyzed 300+ startup deals and drove **$450K+ in AI-informed investments**.
